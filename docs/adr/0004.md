# ADR 0004 · VEX statements never close a finding by themselves

**Status:** Accepted

## Context

Supplier VEX and CSAF documents state that products are not affected by a vulnerability. Applied automatically, a wrong or overly broad statement would silently hide real risk.

## Options considered

1. Apply statements automatically
2. Match statements and let an analyst apply them

## Decision

Statements match a finding only when both the vulnerability and the exact component match. Matches are shown as a preview; an analyst applies them, and the source document is kept with its SHA-256 digest.

## Consequences

- Every not-affected decision has a named person and a source document behind it.
- Ambiguous or conflicting statements are reported and ignored.
