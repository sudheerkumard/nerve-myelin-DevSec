# Security policy — NERvvE DevSecOps

NERvvE DevSecOps Product designed and developed by Sudheer Kumar.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please report suspected vulnerabilities in NERvvE DevSecOps privately:

- **Customers:** through the support channel named in your Order Form, marked "Security".
- **Researchers:** through GitHub's private vulnerability reporting ("Report a vulnerability" under the repository's
  Security tab) for <https://github.com/sudheerkumard/nerve-myelin-DevSec>.

Please do not open public issues for security reports. Include the affected version, the steps to reproduce, the
impact you observed and any proof-of-concept, with secrets and personal data removed.

## What to expect

| Step | Target |
|---|---|
| Acknowledgement | within 3 business days |
| Initial assessment and severity | within 7 business days |
| Fix for critical and high severity issues | as an out-of-cycle release, typically within 30 days |
| Coordinated disclosure | after a fix is available, agreed with the reporter |

## Safe harbour

Good-faith research that follows this policy, respects privacy, avoids service disruption and does not access other
customers' data is welcome and will not be treated as a breach of the Acceptable Use Policy.

## Supported versions

The current release and the previous minor release receive security fixes. See
`docs/legal/SUPPORT-AND-SERVICE-LEVELS.md`.
