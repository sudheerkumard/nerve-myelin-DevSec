# Code of conduct

RAT Flea DevSec is a professional community. Everyone who takes part (in issues, pull requests, discussions and any
other project space) is expected to:

- **Be respectful.** Disagree with ideas, not people. No harassment, insults, or discriminatory language or behaviour.
- **Be constructive.** Explain the problem, propose a solution, and assume good intent.
- **Be responsible.** Never publish anyone's private information, credentials or vulnerability details before they are
  fixed, and follow `SECURITY.md` for security reports.

**Reporting.** If someone's behaviour concerns you, contact the maintainer privately through the contact in
`SECURITY.md`. Reports are handled confidentially.

**Enforcement.** The maintainer may edit or remove contributions, and warn, suspend or ban participants who break this
code of conduct.
